Professional Introduction

I am a B.Sc. Computer Science (Honours) student at Rajagiri College of Social Sciences (Autonomous), pursuing my 4-year Honours degree (2024–2028). I actively build as a Full Stack Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Graphic Designer.

My industry journey began at ERA, an innovative startup incubated inside our college campus, where I worked as a UI/UX Designer creating user experiences, interfaces, and module layouts for platforms like Mentur.

I subsequently transitioned to Developer Lab—which originated as a specialized sub-company under ERA and has now evolved into an independent standalone company. At Developer Lab, I expanded into Graphic Designing and Full Stack Web Development, architecting end-to-end web applications, modern responsive interfaces, and digital branding assets.

I enjoy transforming ideas into functional digital products, designing intuitive user experiences, and developing solutions to real-world problems. I continuously explore new technologies and enhance my technical and creative abilities across the entire stack.
